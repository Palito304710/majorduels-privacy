Privacy Policy

Major Duels  ·  Last updated: August 10, 2026

Major Duels ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. By using the Major Duels app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive an opaque account identifier (a unique ID generated by Google or Apple — not your email address). We also receive the name you have on file with that provider solely as a suggested username, which you may change. We do not store your real name, email address, or password.

Profile information you provide. When setting up your account you choose a username (up to 15 characters), an academic major, a country, and a preferred language. These are stored on our servers.

Gameplay data. We store your ELO rankings, match history, quiz scores, win/loss records, and in-app item balances (gold, lifelines) to power leaderboards and game features.

User-submitted content. If you submit a question or send feedback through the app, the content is emailed to our team for review. Question submissions include your username. Feedback submissions are anonymous.

Device token. If you grant notification permission, we store an Expo push notification token to send you game-related alerts (such as friend challenges). You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Email address or password
  • Precise or approximate GPS location
  • Camera or microphone access
  • Contacts, calendar, or files
  • Health, financial, or government identifier data
  • Browsing history or data from other apps

The country shown on your profile is a user-selected field (pre-filled from your device's locale settings at first launch). We do not access your device's GPS.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account
  • To operate gameplay features (matchmaking, leaderboards, rankings, daily quizzes)
  • To send push notifications for game events you have opted into
  • To review user-submitted questions and feedback to improve the app
  • To process in-app purchases through Apple's payment system

4. Advertising

Major Duels displays ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Privacy Policy). The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers (such as Apple's Advertising Identifier, IDFA) and usage signals to serve and measure ads. This data is collected and processed by Google under their own privacy policy. You can limit ad tracking in your device settings under Privacy → Tracking.

5. In-App Purchases

All in-app purchases (gold packs, Premium subscription) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We receive confirmation from Apple when a purchase is completed, and we credit the corresponding items to your account.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in the following limited cases:

  • Google (OAuth): to verify your identity at login.
  • Apple (OAuth + IAP): to verify your identity at login and to process purchases.
  • Google AdMob: the ad SDK collects device-level signals as described in Section 4.
  • Expo (push notifications): your push token is routed through Expo's notification service to deliver alerts to your device.
  • Legal requirements: if required by law or to protect our rights.

7. Data Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, contact us at jose.rib.tech@gmail.com and we will process your request within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Major Duels is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit and short-lived authentication tokens. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at jose.rib.tech@gmail.com.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
