Delete Your MajorDuels Account

You can request deletion of your MajorDuels account and all associated data at any time using either method below.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

  1. Open the MajorDuels app and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile icon to open your profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account will be permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request via email

Send an email to betpalito3047@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the username or email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 7 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your username and profile information
  • Your match history and ELO ratings
  • Your friends list and friend requests
  • Your in-app purchases history (gold balance, premium status)
  • Your push notification token
  • Any questions you submitted

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Aggregated, anonymised game statistics may be retained.

Note: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel any active subscriptions. Please cancel your Premium subscription through the App Store or Google Play before deleting your account.
